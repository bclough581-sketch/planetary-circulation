# AMALGAM SPINE v1 (locked 2026-11-20)

**One loop, three levels, no conclusions.**

Every feature hangs off this page. A new idea either gets a slot below or goes to the Parking Lot. Nothing gets built without a slot.

## 1. What Amalgam is

A stock-world explorer for everyone, from first-timers to pros. People look, notice, connect and make their own calls. The app points at things worth noticing and keeps an honest score. It never says what to buy or sell.

**The feeling we're after:** "I figured that out." The user earns it; the app doesn't hand it over.

## 2. The loop (the whole app is this)

| Step | What the user does | What the app gives them |
|---|---|---|
| **Look** | Opens the app and scans | Star front door, TRON heatmap, money map |
| **Notice** | Spots something odd | Nudges: "this is unusual" phrased as a question |
| **Connect** | Links two or more things | Web, rings, ripple (if/then) |
| **Postulate** | Writes down an idea and what would prove it wrong | Postulate form, saved to their journal |
| **Watch** | Lets time pass | Signals logged forward every day |
| **Score** | Sees if they were right | Forward scoreboard, journal, streak |

## 3. The three levels (where the loop is played)

| Level | Shows | Built so far |
|---|---|---|
| **World** | Money flows, signals (VIX, credit, claims, liquidity), conflict, weather, shipping | Money map (live), conflict layer (branch), indicator collector (Petunia) |
| **Market** | Sectors and companies as a heatmap, the battle line | Stock World TRON board (branch `stock-world`, sample data) |
| **Company** | One stock taken apart; gates as open logic showing why each rule exists and where it fails | CHECK tab code (kept, unlinked) |

The user zooms in from World to Market to Company. Each level links to the others through the Web.

## 4. How it leads (the algorithms), never concluding

1. **Nudges:** anomalies phrased as questions. Example: "Credit spreads widened 3 days running while small caps rose. What connects them?"
2. **Almanac:** after a postulate, shows "when this lined up before: X of N times," with N always shown. A small N is labeled as thin.
3. **Rings:** the user drags signals into alignment. Close alignment glows; perfect alignment never happens.
4. **Forward score:** every postulate is checked against what actually happened. Being right later is the payoff.

## 5. Learning is the game

- **Unlocks:** the heatmap is open from day one. The first postulate unlocks the Web; 5 scored postulates unlock Rings and the Almanac.
- **The Guide:** a HUD voice that asks questions and explains whatever was tapped, with three settings: teach me, hint, silent.
- **Coffee Briefing:** a 60-second daily routine showing 3 oddities, the streak, and yesterday's postulates scored.
- **Depth on tap:** every number opens to its meaning, then its math, then its source, so beginners and pros use the same screen.

## 6. Hard rules

- No buy/sell wording, no predictions, no personalized advice. Information and education only.
- Sample data is labeled SAMPLE everywhere until real data replaces it.
- Color is never the only signal: every tile shows ▲/▼ and its number, and a color-safe palette is always available.
- Postulates stay on the user's device unless they choose to share them.
- Every data source is license-checked before it goes public. FRED stays private until cleared.
- Respect reduced motion.
- Brent decides merges and deployment.
