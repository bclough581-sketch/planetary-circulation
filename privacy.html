AMALGAM™ PRIVACY

Last updated November 13, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Amalgam™ has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. We do not run a server that receives your data.

What stays on your device

If you use the optional ticker check, your watchlist, holdings, notes, scan log and your own data key are stored in your browser or app storage on your device only. You can clear them at any time by clearing the site's data or removing the app.

Journal records and backups

My decisions saves your decision cards, reasons, evidence, rules, self-reported outcomes and amendments in this browser's storage for this site. The journal does not upload these records to Amalgam™ or send their contents to a third party. Records are not automatically synced to another browser, device or site address. Supported older journal records remain stored separately; copying them into My decisions does not delete the originals.

JSON backups, CSV exports and raw recovery exports contain your private notes. They are created on your device; if downloading is blocked, the journal lets you copy the export text. Exported files or text remain wherever you save or share them. Protect those copies yourself. Deleting My decisions cards does not delete older journal records or exported copies. Clearing this site's browser data can remove local records, so export a backup first if you want to keep them.

Third parties

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you add your own Twelve Data key, ticker symbols you check are sent from your device to Twelve Data to get prices, under their terms. Map data is published to this site from public sources listed on the main page.

Not advice
