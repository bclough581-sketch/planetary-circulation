Sample numbers on a made-up ticker. Not a real stock, not advice. Each ring has lock plates that clamp down one by one as its rule gets closer to being met, and release as the number moves away. As a rule nears, its ring slows and dials toward a notch at the top. When every ring sits on its notch, a beam runs through the sockets, and it never reaches the end. One socket on every ring never engages, because rules being met says nothing about what happens next.
