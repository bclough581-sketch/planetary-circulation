TERMS AND ATTRIBUTION

Source: ECB statistics (euro reference rates). U.S. Treasury (10-year par yield). U.S. Energy Information Administration (WTI and Brent spot). International Monetary Fund, PortWatch (chokepoint transits). Ranges, percentiles, ratios and the season curve are our calculations, not the sources'. Season is a calendar curve, not measured data. Each reading greys out when it is older than its freshness limit (FX and rates 5 days, chokepoints 21) and is left out of the count.

Ring order runs west to east by longitude, starting at the top and moving clockwise (months run January to December).

Color: the WEST and EAST ASIA buttons only swap which color means up and which means down on the market rings. Markets in mainland China, Japan, Korea and Taiwan commonly show red for up.
